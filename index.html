A little about me

Salam...

I am Zabir Saleh—an RF and RAN consultant who turns complex radio networks into clear, reliable systems.

For more than twelve years, I have worked across GSM, WCDMA, LTE and 5G NR: from solution design and capacity planning to integration, modernization, optimization and advanced back-office support.

Born and raised in Riyadh, with roots in Bangladesh and a base in Malaysia, I bring a cross-cultural perspective shaped by projects across EMEA, APAC and the Americas. Beyond telecom delivery, I build software and AI prototypes that make engineering work faster, clearer and more auditable.
