RAN Ops Platform: AI-Driven Unified Digital Twin

A final-year platform concept for bringing network state, operational data and AI-assisted engineering analysis into one explainable RAN operations workspace.

Problem

RAN operations depend on information spread across management systems, performance counters, alarms, topology records and engineering tools. The fragmentation makes correlation slower and limits a clear, shared view of current network state.

Approach

The project explores a unified operational layer that represents network entities and their changing state as a digital twin. AI-assisted analysis sits above that model to help surface anomalies, correlate signals and frame engineering investigation.

The design keeps AI output connected to explainable RAN evidence rather than presenting an unsupported recommendation.

Key features

  • Multi-source network-data ingestion concept
  • Live representation of sites, cells and operational state
  • KPI, event and anomaly visibility
  • AI-assisted correlation and root-cause exploration
  • Role-focused operations views
  • What-if and change-impact analysis concept

Intended outcome

A coherent prototype showing how a digital-twin foundation can improve visibility and provide context for AI-assisted RAN operations and engineering decisions.
