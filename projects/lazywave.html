LazyWave

An AI-driven RAN analysis concept for turning drive-test and RF measurements into engineering interpretation, model comparison and optimization guidance.

Problem

Radio-network measurements are easy to collect but harder to convert into an explainable engineering decision. The project focuses on making ML outputs meaningful in RF terms rather than treating prediction accuracy as the only objective.

Approach

The workflow ingests measurement data, normalizes and encodes features, runs multiple computational-intelligence models and compares their results. The engineering layer then interprets predictions together with RF metrics to support coverage, quality, throughput and root-cause reasoning.

Key features

  • Drive-test and RF measurement ingestion
  • Z-score normalization and categorical encoding
  • Random Forest, Neural Network and Support Vector Machine workflows
  • Model-score comparison and feature interpretation
  • RF performance summary and optimization action prioritization
  • Executive and technical reporting concepts

Technology

Outcome

The key outcome is a framework for comparing model behavior while keeping the final analysis anchored to RF-engineering logic: what degraded, why it likely degraded and what action should be investigated next.
