5G Clustering Study

A clustering study using a 5G QoS dataset to discover patterns across signal strength, latency, bandwidth and resource allocation.

RoleData preparation, clustering and interpretation
StackPython • pandas • scikit-learn

Case study Problem Approach Key features Technology Outcome

Problem

QoS datasets contain multiple interacting dimensions, making it difficult to understand whether users naturally form groups with similar service and resource characteristics.

Approach

The study cleans and transforms the dataset, selects numerical and encoded features, applies clustering algorithms and interprets how resulting groups differ across latency, signal and bandwidth measures.

Key features

  • QoS dataset loading and inspection
  • Feature cleaning and unit conversion
  • Categorical encoding
  • Feature scaling
  • Clustering and cluster-profile analysis
  • Short technical reporting

Technology

Pythonpandasscikit-learnClusteringStandardization5G QoS

Outcome

The analysis shows how unsupervised methods can reveal structure in network-service data even when no predefined label exists.
